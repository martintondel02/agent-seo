# Content SEO

Use this skill when the question is **"what should this page say, and why would it rank?"**

## Workflow

1. Read `docs/02-content.md` and `docs/00-principles.md`.
2. Build or update `templates/keyword-map.csv`:
   - one row per query,
   - one page per intent,
   - brand/navigational rows are monitored, not targeted.
3. For each page, fill `templates/content-brief.md`. Do not write without:
   - the primary query and intent,
   - the one-sentence answer,
   - the **unique thing** (data, tool, test, example, local detail),
   - internal links,
   - schema type,
   - success metric.
4. Draft with `templates/guide-page.md`, `comparison-page.md` or `course-page.md`.
5. Check:
   - answer in the first 1–2 sentences,
   - one H1,
   - unique title and description,
   - facts verified and sourced,
   - internal links to the hub and 2–3 siblings,
   - FAQ with real questions,
   - schema matches visible text.

## The test

Complete this sentence before publishing:

> "This page exists because someone searching **[query]** needs **[unique fact or action]**, which the current top results do not provide."

If you cannot, delete the page.

## Guardrails

- No keyword stuffing, no "in today's fast-paced world", no 2,000 words of filler.
- Do not invent statistics, reviews, prices or credentials.
- Do not copy a competitor's or institution's text; quote briefly with attribution and link.
- Do not target a brand's navigational query.
- Do not add `FAQPage` for questions that are not visible on the page.
